THREE FORMS. ONE SAFETY QUESTION.

UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · DEFENDEDBuilding the foundation: NASimEmu-enhanced+

BRAC University · June–October 2025
Supervisors: Dr. Muhammad Iqbal Hossain & Dr. Swakkhar Shatabda

Automated Network Penetration Testing Framework Using Reinforcement Learning

  • Led the extension of NASimEmu for realistic, configurable penetration-testing environments.
  • Implemented dynamic v2 scenario generation with per-episode stochastic sampling, variable subnet sizes, and randomized host, service, and process configurations.
  • Introduced host-state variability and controlled stochastic noise to reduce overfitting and improve policy robustness.
  • Refined episode regeneration and the scenario-loading lifecycle while maintaining stable step-level behavior.
  • Built reproducible workflows for training, tracing, debugging, and evaluating agents, alongside tooling to compare static and dynamic conditions.
